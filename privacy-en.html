Privacy Policy

Arrived · Last updated 22 November 2026

Overview

Arrived shares location between members of family or friend groups you create or join. This policy explains what we collect, why, how long we keep it, and your rights under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act B.E. 2562 (PDPA). We show no ads, never sell data, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Data controller

The developer of Arrived (Codex Studio). Contact: codex-studio@outlook.com

What we collect

  • Account: email or Sign in with Apple user ID, display name
  • Birth date and gender (optional): only if you choose to enter them during setup or in Edit profile. Stored in your account, visible only to you, never shown to your group; change or clear them any time
  • Location: coordinates, accuracy, speed and time while sharing is on, including in the background
  • Background location: when you are still, the app turns GPS off and iOS wakes it when you leave the area (geofence); while you travel it records your route. Stop sharing any time
  • Device status: battery level, charging state and activity (walking, running, cycling, driving), shown to your group
  • Motion & Fitness (if you allow it): activity type and step counts from your iPhone, used on the device to tell driving from walking and when you got out of the car. Step counts never leave the phone; only the activity label is shared
  • Trip summaries: distance, duration, top/average speed and a driving summary (e.g. hard braking) worked out on the phone when a trip ends
  • Things you enter: saved places, status messages, phone number, emergency card (e.g. blood type, allergies, conditions — sensitive data, stored only if you enter it and consent), emergency contacts
  • Purchases: Apple transaction IDs and expiry dates to unlock Premium (we never see card or payment details)
  • Notifications: your device push token
  • Referrals: your referral code, who invited whom, and credit balance
  • Child account location permission: if a child account turns location off, sets it to "While Using" or turns off Precise Location, the app sends that state (not a location) so the adults in the group can be told
  • Quick messages: the kind of message (e.g. "Tell me when you arrive", "Call me back"), sender, receiver and time
  • On your phone: widgets and Live Activities (Lock Screen / Dynamic Island) keep a copy of your group's names, latest places and status on the device, which may be visible on the Lock Screen; turn them off in iOS settings

How we use it

  • Show your location, status and battery to members of groups you joined
  • Tell the adults in a group when a child account turns off location permission or has sent no location for 12 hours (only between 07:00 and 21:00)
  • Send arrival/departure, SOS and low-battery alerts, and Premium alerts (Smart Arrivals, due-time and speeding alerts)
  • Build the history timeline, time spent at each place and trip summaries your group can view
  • Send live positions phone to phone only while someone in your group is looking at the map (not stored beyond normal history)
  • Verify purchases, calculate referral credit and prevent abuse
  • Birth date and gender, if you enter them, are kept as part of your profile and used only in anonymous aggregate statistics to improve the app — never for advertising

Legal basis

Performance of our service contract (s.24(3)) for data the service needs, and explicit consent (s.19, s.26) for background location and health details in the emergency card. Withdraw consent any time by turning sharing off, clearing the card or deleting your account.

Who sees your data

  • Only members of the groups you join. You can leave or pause sharing any time.
  • You choose whether your group sees your battery, travel activity, timeline and arrive/leave alerts (Me › Security & privacy)
  • Child (supervised) accounts: set by the group admin when inviting or later. A child account can't change what it shares or pause sharing; a parent or guardian should set it up and consent on the child's behalf
  • You can sign in on several phones, but one shares your location. We store a random device ID and the device model for this
  • Processors acting on our instructions: Supabase (database and servers) and Apple (notifications, purchases, maps)
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it
  • We never sell, rent or share data for advertising

International transfers

Our providers' servers may be outside Thailand. We use providers with adequate safeguards and always encrypt data in transit.

Retention

  • Location history: 3 days, or 7 days if you or someone in your group has Premium, then deleted automatically
  • Latest location: until you leave the group or delete your account
  • Alerts and trip summaries in the activity list: 30 days
  • Account data and things you enter: until you delete them or your account
  • Purchase and credit records: as long as law and accounting require

Deleting your account

In the app: Me → Account → Delete account. Your data is deleted immediately and cannot be recovered. Cancel any Premium subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Security

TLS in transit, database Row Level Security so you only see your own groups, encrypted on-device cache, and optional Face ID / Touch ID app lock.

Your rights

You may request access, a copy, correction, deletion, portability, objection or restriction, and withdraw consent, by contacting codex-studio@outlook.com. We reply within 30 days. You may also complain to Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee (PDPC).

Children

  • Users under 20 should use Arrived with a parent's or guardian's consent; for users under 10 a parent or guardian must consent on their behalf.
  • A parent or guardian should install the app on the child's phone, invite the child as a "child account", and explain what the group can see.
  • A child account always sees in the app that it is sharing and who can see it. The app never hides itself.
  • A child account can't change what it shares, pause sharing or leave the group. The group admin can switch it to an adult account or remove it, and the child or a parent can delete the account at any time.
  • We show no ads, build no marketing profiles of children, and sell no one's data.
  • Parents can ask to access, correct or delete their child's data at codex-studio@outlook.com.

Changes

We will tell you in the app before any significant change takes effect.
